ONE CPU 1.1 32

Joshua Luke Nuijens / Axiom 1 Technology. Jane Street protocol emulator. Tiny Tapeout, IHP CMOS5L, tile 8×4. 1724 by 711 µm.

The clock is the step. 50 MHz, one cycle is 20 ns. While the tile is enabled, all 32 bases add 1 to the cycle and 128 to the stride on every rise. That is 1.6 GB/s. The machine does not wait for an instruction, and nothing is fetched.

Nuijens Operational Collapse Mechanics

This tile is that framework. The operational counting, the mathematics, the physics, and the quantum mechanics are mapped into ONE. ONE is the Optimized Numeric Engine: the mapping of the physical mechanics of 1. √2 is the action. Count 1 makes two of the 1 that is already there.

The 32 bases are already loaded. A byte is eight weights, 1 through 128. Base k takes the byte plus k. The seat is 512, held as 4 × 128. The 720 is that same seat in degrees.

ui[0] sits low. Raise it for one cycle, then let it fall. That cycle is FIRE. The byte is already on uio[7:0]. On that cycle all 32 bases take it. Base 0 gets the byte. Base k gets the byte plus k.

On the die

  • Any of 256 bytes. 113 machines were fired, 185 cycles. Every byte came back as itself.
  • UART at 100 Mbit/s, SPI mode 0, and I2C. The map picks the pins. The hold picks the bit time: 1, 4, 5, 8, 16, 33, 125, or 434 clocks.
  • On map 11: the memory, JTAG, Manchester, SWD, PS/2, a USB low-speed bit, and a CAN bit.
  • A register stores the map, the hold, and the byte after the chip is made. Later cycles follow the register.
  • Two front memories hold a list of 1,024 of those steps.
  • Two back memories store a cycle each. On FIRE the pins show the new one minus the one before it.
  • Add, carry, the stride as the multiply, the hold as the divide, AND, OR, XOR, shift, and compare.
  • The seat is 512. The 720 is that number times 720/512. r15 stays 4F4E4553.

The byte

A byte is eight weights: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 128. The 1s are the ones in the sum. 90 is 10010000, weights 16 and 128, result 104F0090. The low byte of the result is the byte that went in. All 256 do this. A wider instruction is its bytes, one cycle each.
